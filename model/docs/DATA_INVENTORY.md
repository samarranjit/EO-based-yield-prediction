## Label units & provenance
**bu/ac**, confirmed from every raster's own band description
(`pseudo_yield_bu_ac`). Multiply by `BU_AC_TO_KG_HA` (67.251) for kg/ha. Earlier
revisions of this file said kg/ha — that was wrong. `label_units: bu_ac` is a
reporting label only and does not affect training or normalisation.

**`label_source = ridge_distributed_county_yield`** — county NASS yield
redistributed to 30 m. These are **pseudo-pixel labels**, not measured 30 m yield.
See LOYO_PROTOCOL.md for the leakage caveat and DECISION_LOG.md ("Findings that
constrain future design") for exactly what the redistribution does and does not
encode: within-county structure is a monotone function of one fixed 4-VI linear
projection, with no soil or management term.

Measured 30 m yield exists **only** for BARC
(`../data_preparation/data/barc_data/yield_dataset/`, 2014–2024). See
BARC_TRANSFER.md.

## No-data conventions
Label −9999 → NaN; CDL 0/1; validity never from `yield==0`.

## Suspected issues / notes
- **HLS imagery is on disk** (`../data_preparation/data/HLS_Composites/{year}/hls_soybeans_{ST}_{YEAR}_{MON}.tif`)
  and real batches have trained on the real 600M backbone. The earlier "imagery
  absent" note is stale.
- **Corrupt DEFLATE tiles**: some HLS files raise only when decoded, inside a
  DataLoader worker, killing a run hours in. Affected chips are listed per
  experiment in `data.exclude_sample_ids`; scans live in `outputs/qc/`
  (`scripts/scan_hls_corruption.py`). The 2014–2024 5-state scan found 82 bad
  chips, 78 of them in two MD 2015 files from one export batch. See
  TROUBLESHOOTING.md.
- The primary experiments use `label_variant: pseudo` with
  `label_root: ../data_preparation/data/yield_labels/multistate_pseudo`. The older
  `bilinear` / `county_values` variants are superseded.
- Prince George's County MD (GEOID 24033 / state MD) is the BARC site and is
  excluded from national labels by the upstream pipeline; keep it out of national
  training (`data.exclude_geoids`).
- No duplicate label/CDL files detected; all state-years unique.

## Distinguishing tested-on-synthetic vs tested-on-real
- **Real data, real backbone**: full training runs under `outputs/runs/` —
  `cornbelt5_soybeans_longer_time` (reference county model, `farm-018`), BARC
  11-fold LOYO transfer incl. `refiner_only`, and zero-shot spatial holdouts on
  MO/NE. Raster metadata/alignment, inventory and manifest enumeration likewise
  verified against on-disk rasters.
- **Synthetic + dummy backbone**: the fast CI path only —
  `configs/experiments/smoke_dummy.yaml` and the `not integration` test suite
  (dataset shapes, forward/backward, losses, metrics, augmentation, LOYO audit,
  tiled mosaic). Use it to sanity-check pipeline changes, not to claim results.
