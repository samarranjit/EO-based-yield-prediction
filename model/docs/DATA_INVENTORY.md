## Label units & provenance
kg/ha. **`label_source = ridge_distributed_county_yield`** — county NASS yield
redistributed to 30 m by an external ridge/bilinear procedure. These are
**pseudo-pixel labels**, not measured 30 m yield. See LOYO_PROTOCOL.md for the
leakage caveat.

## No-data conventions
Label −9999 → NaN; CDL 0/1; validity never from `yield==0`.

## Suspected issues / notes
- **HLS imagery absent** → no real batch has passed through the model yet;
  synthetic + real-label/CDL checks only.
- Two label variants exist (`bilinear`, `county_values`); the primary experiment
  uses `bilinear10km`.
- Prince George's County MD (GEOID 24033 / state MD) is the BARC site and is
  excluded from national labels by the upstream pipeline; keep it out of national
  training (`data.exclude_geoids`).
- No duplicate label/CDL files detected; all state-years unique.

## Distinguishing tested-on-synthetic vs tested-on-real
- **Real data**: raster metadata/alignment, inventory, manifest window
  enumeration (e.g. DE 2018 → 345 chips) — verified against on-disk rasters.
- **Synthetic data**: dataset shapes, model forward/backward, losses, metrics,
  augmentation, LOYO audit, tiled mosaic, training loop — verified with the
  synthetic dataset + dummy backbone. **No real-imagery training was run.**
